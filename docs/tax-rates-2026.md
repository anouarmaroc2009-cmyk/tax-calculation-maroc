# Tax Rates 2026 — Morocco (CGI 2026)

## 1. Corporate Income Tax (IS — Impôt sur les Sociétés)

### Standard Rates (Art. 19-I, effective FY 2026+)

| Category | Net Taxable Profit | Rate |
|----------|-------------------|------|
| Standard companies | < 100M MAD | **20%** |
| Large companies | ≥ 100M MAD | **35%** |
| Credit institutions, Bank Al-Maghrib, CDG, Insurance/Reinsurance | Any | **40%** |
| CFC (Casablanca Finance City) companies | Any | **20%** |
| IAZ (Industrial Acceleration Zones) companies | Any | **20%** |
| Companies with investment agreement ≥ 1.5B MAD | Any | **20%** |
| Microfinance (SA, first 5 years post-transition) | < 100M MAD / ≥ 100M MAD | **20% / 35%** |

### Minimum Contribution (Cotisation Minimale — Art. 144-I)

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rate | **0.25%** of (Operating Revenue + Financial Income + Subsidies) |
| Minimum | **3,000 MAD** |
| Exemption | First 36 months of activity |
| Reduced rate (petroleum, gas, butter, oil, sugar, flour, water, electricity, meds) | **0.15%** |

### Quarterly Installments (Art. 247-XXXVII-B)

- 4 installments × 25% of prior-year IS (or MC if higher)
- Due: end of M3, M6, M9, M12 following fiscal year opening
- Transitional rule (2023-2026): installments based on current-year rate

### Loss Carry-Forward (Art. 10-I-F-2°)

| Type | Period |
|------|--------|
| Ordinary losses | 4 years |
| Loss portion related to depreciation | **Unlimited** |
| Carry-back | Not permitted |

### Withholding Tax on Dividends (Art. 73-II-B-7°)

| Period | Rate |
|--------|------|
| From January 1, 2025 | 12.50% |
| From January 1, 2026 | 11.25% |
| From January 1, 2027 (target) | **10%** |

## 2. Individual Income Tax (IR — Impôt sur le Revenu)

### Progressive Scale 2026 (Art. 73)

| Net Taxable Income Bracket (MAD/year) | Marginal Rate | Deduction (MAD) |
|---------------------------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| 0 – 30,000 | **0%** | 0 |
| 30,001 – 50,000 | **10%** | 3,000 |
| 50,001 – 60,000 | **20%** | 8,000 |
| 60,001 – 80,000 | **30%** | 14,000 |
| 80,001 – 180,000 | **34%** | 17,200 |
| > 180,000 | **38%** | 24,400 |

**Formula**: `IR = (RNI × Rate) - Deduction`

### Monthly Scale (for payroll withholding)

| Monthly Net Taxable Income | Rate | Deduction |
|---------------------------|------|-----------|
| 0 – 3,333.33 | 0% | 0 |
| 3,333.34 – 5,000 | 10% | 333.33 |
| 5,000.01 – 6,666.67 | 20% | 833.33 |
| 6,666.68 – 8,333.33 | 30% | 1,500 |
| 8,333.34 – 15,000 | 34% | 1,833.33 |
| > 15,000 | 38% | 2,283.33 |

### Income Categories (Art. 36)

| # | Category | Taxation Method |
|---|----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Salaires** (Salaries) | Withheld at source (mono-employer); global IR (multi-employer) |
| 2 | **Revenus Professionnels** (Professional) | RNR, RNS, or CPU regime |
| 3 | **Revenus Agricoles** (Agricultural) | Flat-rate or actual regime |
| 4 | **Revenus Fonciers** (Real Estate) | 10% (< 120K) or 15% (≥ 120K) WHT, non-final (Art. 57) |
| 5 | **Revenus des Capitaux Mobiliers** (Movable Capital) | Various final/creditable WHT |
| 6 | **Plus-values** (Capital Gains) | Flat rates |

### Withholding Tax Rates on Investment Income

| Income Type | Rate | Nature | Legal Basis |
|------------|------|--------|-------------|
| Dividends (Moroccan companies) | 10% | Final WHT | Art. 73-II-B-7° |
| Dividends (foreign source) | 15% | Declarable | Art. 73-II-C-2° |
| Interest (individual, non-professional) | 30% | Final WHT | Art. 73-II-G-3° |
| Interest (professional RNR/RNS) | 20% | Creditable WHT | Art. 73-II-F-1° |
| Rental income (gross < 120K/year) | 10% | Non-final WHT | Art. 57 |
| Rental income (gross ≥ 120K/year) | 15% | Non-final WHT | Art. 57 |
| Listed share capital gains | 15% | Flat | Art. 73 |
| Unlisted share capital gains | 20% | Flat | Art. 73 |
| Real estate capital gains | 20% (min 3% sale price) | Flat | Art. 73 |
| Foreign-source capital gains | 20% | Flat | Art. 73 |

### Professional Income Regimes

| Regime | Applicable To | Tax Base |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| **RNR** (Régime du Résultat Net Réel) | CA > 2M MAD | Actual profit (accounting) |
| **RNS** (Régime du Net Simplifié) | CA 500K-2M MAD | Revenue - fixed % deduction |
| **CPU** (Classe des Professions Libérales Unifiée) | Liberal professions | Simplified categories |

### Family Charge Reduction (Art. 74)

- Base reduction per dependent
- Applied after gross IR calculation
- Capped at specified limits per Finance Law

## 3. Value Added Tax (TVA — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée)

### Rates 2026 (Post-Reform, Art. 99)

| Rate | Category | Examples |
|------|----------|---------|
| **20%** | Standard | Most goods & services, electricity, domestic air/sea transport, non-urban freight transport, rail transport |
| **10%** | Reduced (with deduction right) | Banking services, hotels & restaurants, urban passenger transport, road freight, refined sugar, edible oils, rice, husked rice, pasta (long), renewable energy equipment, water distribution |
| **10%** | Reduced (without deduction right) | Insurance brokers, direct marketers |
| **0% / Exempt (ADD)** | Exempt with deduction right | Exports |
| **Exempt (SDD)** | Exempt without deduction right | Bread, couscous, semolina, domestic water, essential medicines, books/journals, short pasta (non-cooked, non-filled from Jan 2026), fertilizing materials (from Jan 2026) |

### Filing Regimes

| Regime | Threshold | Filing Frequency | Deadline |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Monthly | CA > 1M MAD/year | Monthly | 20th of following month |
| Quarterly | CA ≤ 1M MAD/year | Quarterly | 20th of month following quarter |
| Exempt | CA < 500K MAD (commercial/industrial) | Not required | N/A |

### Auto-Liquidation (Self-Invoicing)

New in 2026: Industrial transformation companies must self-invoice VAT on:
- Industrial waste/scrap
- Metals and recovery materials

### VAT Deduction Rights Matrix

| Input Category | Deduction Right |
|---------------|----------------|
| Goods/services used for taxable operations | Full deduction |
| Goods/services used for exempt operations (SDD) | No deduction |
| Mixed-use | Pro-rata deduction |
| Passenger vehicles (personal use component) | Partial/capped |
| Entertainment & hospitality | Capped deduction |
